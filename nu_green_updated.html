🔬 SCIENCE-BACKED

Research-Proven Impact

NU Green is built on evidence-based sustainability and wellbeing practices

🪴 Biophilic Design Works

50 plants in a classroom dramatically improved students' perceived environmental quality, air freshness, and productivity.

20% productivity increase in workspaces with biophilic design over 15 years.

Sources: Building and Environment Journal (2024) • Paharpur Study, India

🌱 NU Grow Gardens Deliver

Community gardens yield 0.75 lbs per square foot. Every $1 invested returns $6 in produce value.

Students save an average of $435 per season growing their own food.

Sources: UC Agriculture & Natural Resources • USDA Research

💚 Mental Health Matters

At northern campuses like NU Seattle and Boston, 10% of students experience Seasonal Affective Disorder—double the national 5% rate.

Biophilic design reduces stress, improves self-esteem, and enhances cognitive function.

Sources: Oregon State University • NIH/PMC • American Psychiatric Association

🧑‍🎓 Student Wellbeing

80-90% of student life happens indoors. Access to green spaces creates restorative learning environments.

Natural light and plants reduce anxiety and improve concentration and academic performance.

Source: NIH/PMC - Biophilic Design on College Campuses
