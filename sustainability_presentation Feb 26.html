What goes where? 🗑️

Three bins. Three simple rules. Let's make tonight a zero-confusion zone.

🖤
Trash
Black / Grey Bin
  • Chip bags & plastic wrap
  • Used paper towels & napkins
  • Styrofoam cups & containers
  • Soiled/greasy packaging
  • Mixed material wrappers
When in doubt, throw it out here.
💚
Compost
Green Bin
  • All food scraps & leftovers
  • Paper napkins & paper plates
  • Compostable containers
  • Fruit & veggie peels
  • Coffee grounds & tea bags
Food waste = compost, not trash!
💙
Recycle
Blue Bin
  • Clean plastic bottles & jugs
  • Glass bottles & jars
  • Cardboard & clean paper
  • Aluminum & steel cans
  • Empty cartons (rinsed)
Must be clean & dry to recycle!

Small actions,
big impact.

Every correct bin toss tonight is a tiny win for our planet. You've got this.
